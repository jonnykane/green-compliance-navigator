## 1. What is the CSRD?

The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (EU Directive 2022/2464) entered into force on 5 January 2023. It replaces the Non-Financial Reporting Directive (NFRD) and significantly expands the scope, detail, and assurance requirements for sustainability reporting across the EU.

The CSRD requires in-scope companies to report on environmental, social, and governance (ESG) matters using the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS), developed by the European Financial Reporting Advisory Group (EFRAG). Reporting must appear in a dedicated section of companies' management reports and must be independently assured.

Although the UK is no longer an EU member state, the CSRD is directly relevant to many UK-headquartered businesses. This document sets out when and how.

---

## 2. Does CSRD Apply to UK Companies?

The CSRD does not automatically apply to UK companies by virtue of UK law. However, it applies in two distinct ways:

### 2.1 UK Companies with EU Subsidiaries or EU-Listed Securities

A UK-based parent company falls into CSRD scope under Article 40 (the "third-country undertaking" provision) if:

- It generated **net turnover exceeding €150 million** in the EU in each of the last two consecutive financial years, **AND**
- It has at least one of the following EU-based entities:
  - A **large EU subsidiary** (meeting the large company thresholds — see Section 3)
  - An EU-regulated-market-**listed subsidiary** (regardless of size)
  - An EU **branch** generating net turnover exceeding €40 million in the previous financial year

Where these conditions are met, the **EU subsidiary or branch** is required to produce a sustainability report covering the entire **group** (including the UK parent). The UK parent does not file directly, but must provide its EU subsidiary with the necessary data.

### 2.2 UK Companies Listed on EU Regulated Markets

A UK company whose securities are listed on an EU regulated market (for example, Euronext Amsterdam or Frankfurt Stock Exchange) is in scope as a **third-country undertaking with EU-listed securities** from the financial year beginning 1 January 2028 (reporting in 2029), subject to the large company test.

---

## 3. What Counts as a "Large Company" Under the CSRD?

The CSRD uses the EU Accounting Directive definition. A company is "large" if it exceeds **at least two of the following three thresholds** on its balance sheet date:

| Threshold | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Balance sheet total | More than €20 million |
| Net turnover | More than €40 million |
| Average number of employees | More than 250 |

For listed SMEs, different (lighter) requirements and a later timeline apply (see Section 4).

---

## 4. Phased Implementation Timeline

| Wave | Scope | First financial year | First reporting year |
|------|-------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Wave 1 | Large EU public-interest entities already subject to NFRD (>500 employees) | 1 January 2024 | 2025 |
| Wave 2 | All other large EU companies (meeting 2 of 3 thresholds above) | 1 January 2025 | 2026 |
| Wave 3 | Listed SMEs on EU regulated markets; small, non-complex credit institutions; captive insurers | 1 January 2026 | 2027 (with opt-out to 2028) |
| Wave 4 | Third-country undertakings (incl. UK parents) with net EU turnover >€150m and qualifying EU subsidiary/branch | 1 January 2028 | 2029 |

**Important:** Member states may allow listed SMEs to opt out until financial years beginning 1 January 2028.

---

## 5. What Must Be Reported?

In-scope companies must report on sustainability matters under the **double materiality** principle: both the impact of sustainability matters on the company (financial materiality) and the company's impact on society and the environment (impact materiality).

Reporting covers:

- **Environmental:** Climate change (mitigation, adaptation, Scope 1/2/3 GHG emissions), pollution, water and marine resources, biodiversity and ecosystems, resource use and circular economy
- **Social:** Own workforce, workers in the value chain, affected communities, consumers and end-users
- **Governance:** Business conduct, anti-corruption and anti-bribery, lobbying, payment practices

Reporting must comply with the **European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS)**. The cross-cutting standards (ESRS 1 and ESRS 2) are mandatory; topical standards are subject to materiality assessment.

---

## 6. Assurance Requirements

CSRD reports require **limited assurance** by an accredited independent auditor or assurance service provider from Wave 1. The European Commission may move to **reasonable assurance** in subsequent phases.

UK companies providing data to EU subsidiaries for group-level CSRD reports should note that the data they supply will ultimately be subject to this assurance requirement.

---

## 7. Relationship to Other UK Frameworks

| Framework | Relationship to CSRD |
|-----------|---------------------|
| TCFD (FCA mandatory) | ESRS climate disclosure (ESRS E1) broadly aligns with TCFD; companies compliant with FCA TCFD rules will have a foundation but CSRD requires more detail |
| SECR | SECR energy and carbon data satisfies some ESRS E1 inputs but does not constitute CSRD compliance |
| UK SDS (forthcoming) | UK SDS is based on ISSB standards, not ESRS; UK and EU standards will diverge; companies with dual obligations will need to manage both |

---

## 8. Key Questions for UK Businesses

**Do we have an EU subsidiary?** If yes, check whether it qualifies as "large" under the EU thresholds. If so, your EU subsidiary may be in scope from Wave 2 (financial year 2025) and will need group-level sustainability data from you.

**What is our EU turnover?** If your group's EU net turnover exceeded €150 million in each of the last two financial years, and you have a qualifying EU subsidiary or branch, you are in scope under Article 40 from 2028.

**Are we listed on an EU regulated market?** If so, UK-headquartered status does not exempt you from the third-country obligations.

**Does our EU subsidiary file under Wave 1 or Wave 2?** Wave 1 companies (former NFRD in-scope entities, >500 employees) are already reporting for FY2024. Wave 2 companies begin for FY2025.
